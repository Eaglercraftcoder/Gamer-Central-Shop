Our History

In 2015, a guy named Rehman Faisal had an awesome idea! He looked at his two favorite things in the whole world—video games and cool clothes—and thought, "Hey, what if I smash these together to bring gamers everywhere into one big, friendly team?" And just like that, Gamer Central was born!
It started out as a neat website, but it quickly grew into something way bigger. Rehman didn't just want to sell shirts and hats; he wanted to build the ultimate hangout spot.
Today, it’s a massive, happy community where people from all over the world can connect, share their highest scores, and wear awesome gear that shows off their love for gaming.
It proves that video games aren't just for playing by yourself in your room—they are the perfect way to make brand-new best friends!

Why We Do It

We do this because we believe video games are way more fun when you play together! Sometimes, people think gaming is just about sitting by yourself staring at a screen. But we know the truth: video games are like a bridge that can connect anyone, anywhere!
No matter what games you like to play, what high scores you get, or where you live, you have a place on our team.
We think clothing is like a real-life avatar skin! Our cool clothes let you show the whole world what you love without saying a single word.
